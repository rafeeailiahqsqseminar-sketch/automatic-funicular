# Terms of Service — Jadual TikTok Rafee

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Jadual TikTok Rafee ("the App") is a personal scheduling tool operated by Rafee Ailiah for his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

1. **Use.** The App lets its owner schedule his own videos through a private Telegram bot and publish them to his own TikTok account at the scheduled time using the TikTok Content Posting API.
2. **Authorization.** The App only acts on a TikTok account after the account owner signs in with TikTok and grants permission. The owner can revoke access at any time in the bot (`/putustiktok`) or in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services).
3. **Content.** The account owner is responsible for every video and caption he schedules, and for following the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
4. **No warranty.** The App is provided "as is". A post may fail or be delayed; when that happens the bot sends a reminder so the owner can post manually.
