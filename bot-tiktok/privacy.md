# Privacy Policy — Jadual TikTok Rafee

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Jadual TikTok Rafee ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its owner, Rafee Ailiah, to publish videos to his own TikTok account.

## Data the App uses

- **TikTok:** the access token and refresh token issued by TikTok after sign-in, the account's open ID and display name (scope `user.info.basic`), and permission to publish videos (scope `video.publish`).
- **Telegram:** the videos and captions the owner sends to his private Telegram bot, and the chat ID needed to reply to him.

## How it is used

- The data is used only to publish the owner's scheduled videos to his TikTok account and to tell him whether each post succeeded.
- TikTok tokens are stored encrypted. They are never sold, shared or used for advertising.
- No data from any other TikTok user is collected.

## Retention and deletion

- Schedule records are deleted automatically 14 days after a video is posted.
- Sending `/putustiktok` to the bot deletes the stored TikTok tokens and revokes access. Access can also be removed in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.
